Meet Nova

Nova is a privately operated personal artificial intelligence assistant designed to help its owner manage daily activities, information, and connected services.

Nova combines conversational AI with authorized integrations to provide a personalized assistant experience.

Google Workspace Integration

Nova uses Google OAuth authorization to access selected Google services, including Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Tasks.

These integrations support email management, scheduling, reminders, and task organization.

Access requires explicit authorization from the Google account owner.

Privacy & Security

Nova is intended for private, personal use and is not offered as a publicly accessible AI service.

Google account information is accessed only through permissions granted by the account owner. Nova does not sell personal information or use Google account data for advertising.

See the Privacy Policy for additional information.

Availability

Nova is a personal project and is not available for public registration or general use.
