Nova Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Overview

Nova Assistant is a privately operated personal artificial intelligence assistant designed for use by its authorized owner. Nova is not offered as a public service.

2. Information Accessed

With authorization, Nova may access information from:

  • Gmail: Messages, message metadata, and email content.
  • Google Calendar: Calendar events and scheduling information.
  • Google Tasks: Task lists, tasks, and associated details.

Nova accesses Google information only through permissions granted by the Google account owner.

3. How Information Is Used

Google account information is used to provide personal assistant functionality, including:

  • Retrieving and organizing email information.
  • Managing calendar events and schedules.
  • Creating, reviewing, and updating tasks.
  • Responding to authorized requests from the account owner.

4. AI Processing and Data Sharing

Nova may transmit information to configured AI service providers when necessary to process authorized requests. Such processing depends on the AI models and services configured by the owner.

Nova does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google user data is not shared for advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

Nova operates primarily through privately managed computing infrastructure.

Google authorization credentials and relevant application data may be stored on systems controlled by the owner. Access to these systems is restricted.

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect stored information and authorization credentials.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained as needed to provide assistant functionality, maintain application state, and support authorized operations.

The owner can delete locally stored application data and revoke Google account access.

7. Revoking Google Access

Google account permissions can be revoked through the Google Account security settings.

Revoking access prevents Nova from obtaining additional Google account information using the revoked authorization. Previously stored information may require separate deletion.

8. Limited Personal Use

Nova Assistant is intended solely for private, authorized personal use.

There is no public account registration or commercial distribution of this application.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to Nova's integrations, functionality, or data handling.
